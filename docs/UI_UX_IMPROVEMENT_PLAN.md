# GhayMajha Ultra-Premium Luxury UI/UX Elevation Plan

---

## 1. Aesthetic Identity & Colour Palette
To match high-end European luxury ateliers (Hermès, Leica, Bottega Veneta, Porsche Design):
- **Warm Ivory:** `#F8F6F1` (Canvas & primary card backgrounds)
- **Soft Cream:** `#F1EDE5` (Secondary section surface & borders)
- **Deep Charcoal:** `#171717` (High-contrast typography)
- **Rich Black:** `#0B0B0B` (Buttons, navigation chrome, high-end badges)
- **Muted Champagne Gold:** `#B49A6A` (Accent highlights, star ratings, premium borders)
- **Warm Taupe:** `#A69B8B` (Secondary text, breadcrumbs, subtle dividers)
- **Subtle Line:** `#E7E1D8` (Clean, hair-thin container dividers)

---

## 2. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Editorial Headings:** Serif styling with relaxed, confident tracking (`font-serif`, tracking `-0.02em` to `-0.04em`).
- **Body & Product Metadata:** Clean, modern sans-serif (`font-sans`, line-height `1.65`, comfortable reading size).
- **Technical & SKU Callouts:** Refined monospaced font (`font-mono`, subtle opacity).

---

## 3. Storefront UI Enhancements
1. **Announcement Bar:**
   - Subtle champagne gold border, understated typography, announcing complimentary shipping on orders over ₹1,499.
2. **Hero Presentation:**
   - Editorial typography with balanced proportions, refined action buttons, and fluid responsive spacing.
3. **Product Cards:**
   - Soft hover zoom transitions on product photography.
   - Clean badges (`Bestseller`, `New`, `Featured`) styled in ivory/black.
   - Responsive "Quick Add" action with smooth status transformation.
4. **Product Detail Page (PDP):**
   - High-clarity multi-image gallery with sticky product details.
   - Magnification lens with smooth hardware-accelerated transforms.
   - Elegant accordion specs and fit guarantee notes.
5. **Customer Bag & Checkout:**
   - Clean line item layout with actual photo thumbnails.
   - Hair-thin dividers, transparent pricing summary, and frictionless Razorpay modal activation.
6. **Public Order Tracking:**
   - Polished visual timeline (Placed → Confirmed → Processing → Packed → Shipped → Delivered) with real status milestones.

---

## 4. Admin Mission-Control Polish
1. **Executive Overview:**
   - Stat cards with muted champagne and charcoal accents.
   - Visual revenue metrics and recent orders table with status badges.
2. **Product & Image Manager:**
   - Visual grid showing multiple attached photos with Cover Photo designation and quick re-ordering.
3. **Order Inspection & Invoicing:**
   - Printable luxury packing slip and invoice (`/admin/orders/[id]/invoice`) with brand watermark.
